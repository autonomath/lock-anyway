Plain-language policy

Privacy

Lock Anyway is a local-only Mac utility. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data collection

None. No analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud service, or network feature.

Keyboard and mouse

Lock Anyway reads the macOS idle-time counter: the seconds since local keyboard or mouse activity. It never captures keys, clicks, or screen contents.

Battery

Low-battery alerts read the battery level and charger state from macOS. That reading stays on your Mac.

Local storage

Your preferences are stored on your Mac. The local log records app state and errors, never documents or input events.

Accessibility

Lock Anyway locks your session directly. It asks for Accessibility only on systems where it must press Control–Command–Q instead, and never uses it to inspect other apps.

The gap it closes

Keep awake
≠ stay unlocked.

Videos, meetings, remote sessions, and downloads can keep your Mac awake for hours.

Lock Anyway asks one question: has anyone touched the keyboard or mouse? If not, it locks on your schedule.

Questions, answered

The useful details.

Why not just use the built-in Lock Screen settings?+

If macOS already locks when you expect, you don't need it. Lock Anyway is a backstop for when media, remote access, or another app keeps the display awake.

Does it read what I type?+

No. It reads only the seconds since your last input, never keys, clicks, or screen contents.

Why might macOS ask for Accessibility permission?+

Usually it won't. Lock Anyway locks your session directly and asks for Accessibility only on systems where it must press Control–Command–Q instead.

Will downloads or remote sessions keep running?+

Yes. Locking doesn't quit any apps. Whether the Mac stays awake afterward depends on macOS and the app keeping it awake.

Is it open source?+

Yes, under the MIT License. Read the source, build it yourself, or report issues on GitHub.
